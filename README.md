# Dexter
- **Season 4** was one of my favorite. I cannot skip this under any circumstances. John Lithgow as the Trinity Killer gives me the best season of the entire series, culminating in a jaw-dropping finale that changes everything.

# Avengers
- Best movie for me is **Endgame**, part of what makes **Endgame** a favorite is the communal magic of it. Hearing a crowded theater erupt in pure, deafening cheers when Captain America wields Thor's hammer, or when Sam Wilson says "On your left" before every portal opens up, is a moviegoing memory I will never forget.
